## Paywall rock-hand artwork

The rock-hand image on the paywall (`HiddenJams/Assets.xcassets/RockHand.imageset`)
is the emoji PNG provided by Chris himself (2026-10-14). The checkerboard
background baked into the JPEG he sent was stripped to transparency so the hand
displays cleanly; the hand artwork is his file, pixel-for-pixel.
