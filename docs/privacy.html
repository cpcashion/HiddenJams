Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Hidden Jams ("the app") is developed by Chris Cashion. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

What the app does with your information

To find music for you, Hidden Jams connects to your Spotify account (with your explicit permission) and reads your profile, top tracks, and library. It analyzes this listening data on your device to discover obscure artists you'll love, and plays 30-second previews provided by Spotify, Deezer, and Apple (iTunes Search).

  • Spotify data: accessed through Spotify's official API under your Spotify account and Spotify's own privacy policy. The app cannot see your Spotify password.
  • On your device: your login tokens, preferences, and discovery history are stored privately on your iPhone (in the app's local storage) and are never uploaded to our servers — we operate no servers.
  • AI taste profile: if you generate a taste profile, anonymized listening statistics (genres, artists, eras — no name, email, or account identifiers) may be sent to OpenAI's API to produce the description. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Artist info: the app looks up public artist data from Last.fm and MusicBrainz.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) is used.
  • No cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We collect no analytics about you on our own systems.

Your choices

You can disconnect Spotify at any time from your profile in the app, which deletes the stored login from your device. You can also revoke Hidden Jams' access at spotify.com/account/apps.

Children

Hidden Jams is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
