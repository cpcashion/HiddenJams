Hidden Jams Support

Hidden Jams finds the music the algorithms won't show you. Connect your Spotify library and we'll dig through underground and obscure artists to surface hidden gems matched to your taste.

FAQ

How do I connect my Spotify account?

Open Hidden Jams and tap Connect with Spotify. You'll be taken to Spotify to approve access, then returned to the app. We only request permission to read your library and top tracks — never to change anything without asking.

A track plays no audio. What happened?

Some tracks don't have a playable 30-second preview from our preview providers. Hidden Jams automatically skips those and moves to the next playable track.

How do I save a discovery?

Tap the heart on any track to save it. Your favorites are collected in your Hidden Jams playlist, which you can export straight to Spotify.

How do I disconnect Spotify?

Go to your profile in Hidden Jams and choose Disconnect Spotify. This removes the stored login from your device. You can also revoke access anytime at spotify.com/account/apps.

Is my listening data sold or shared for ads?

No. Hidden Jams shows no ads, performs no ad tracking, and sells no data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
